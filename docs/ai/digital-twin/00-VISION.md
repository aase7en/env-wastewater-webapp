# Environmental Operations Digital Twin — Vision

Status: APPROVED DIRECTION

## Product Vision

Create a living operational representation of hospital environmental systems, beginning with wastewater treatment.

The product combines:

- trustworthy operational records
- optional stylized 3D spatial understanding
- existing schematic/process views
- trends, reports, alerts, history, and audit
- future AI-assisted operations

It is not a game and not a 3D replacement for existing workflows.

## Experience Direction

The 3D world may be warm, approachable, and low-poly. The surrounding application remains professional, calm, and scannable through the UTH[AI]-ENV Aura design system.

Memorable site-specific detail for the wastewater twin:

- warm concrete tanks
- turquoise PVC pipes
- red ladders/handles
- blue Thai tank signs
- green lawn and hedges
- the canal as distant spatial context

## Truth Rules

- Unknown is not zero.
- Unknown is not normal.
- Unknown is not stopped.
- Manual daily data is not LIVE.
- A visual detail may describe physical structure without claiming an operational state.
- A state animation requires a semantic field from the Twin contract or an explicitly labeled scenario override.

## Long-Term Domains

- Wastewater Treatment
- Solar Energy
- Water System
- Waste Management
- Air Quality
- Building Environment

Only the Aeration Tank vertical slice is active in the current phase.
